# HoopMind — Usability and User Satisfaction Survey

Ask each participant to use the deployed HoopMind chatbot, then complete this survey.
Do not count your own developer test as a participant response.

## Part A — Task success
Mark Yes or No after the participant tries each task.

1. Greet the chatbot and end the conversation.
2. Ask what NBA data HoopMind can provide.
3. Ask for a player's statistic in a specific season.
4. Ask for information about a player.
5. Ask about a player's awards.
6. Ask who won a specific award in a year.
7. Ask about a team's information or statistics.
8. Ask about draft information.
9. Ask whether a player was an All-Star.
10. Compare two players or two teams.

Task success rate = successful tasks / 10 × 100%.

## Part B — System Usability Scale (SUS)
Rate each statement from 1 (Strongly disagree) to 5 (Strongly agree).

1. I think that I would like to use this chatbot frequently.
2. I found the chatbot unnecessarily complex.
3. I thought the chatbot was easy to use.
4. I think that I would need the help of a technical person to use this chatbot.
5. I found the chatbot functions well integrated.
6. I thought there was too much inconsistency in the chatbot.
7. I would imagine that most people would learn to use the chatbot quickly.
8. I found the chatbot cumbersome to use.
9. I felt confident using the chatbot.
10. I needed to learn many things before I could use the chatbot.

## Part C — User satisfaction
Rate 1 (Very poor) to 5 (Excellent).

- Overall satisfaction
- Accuracy of answers
- Relevance and quality of responses
- Response speed
- Ability to understand differently phrased questions
- Likelihood of recommending HoopMind

Optional comments: What did you like? What should be improved?
